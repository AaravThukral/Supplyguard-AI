# Paper 1 - Supplier Selection: A Multi-Criteria Decision Problem

## Paper Information

**Title**

Application of Decision-Making Techniques in Supplier Selection: A Systematic Review of Literature

**Authors**

Chai, Liu, Ng

**Year**

2013

**Journal**

Expert Systems with Applications

---

# Research Objective

The objective of this paper is to study how organizations select suppliers and review the techniques researchers have used over the years.

Instead of proposing a new model, the paper summarizes existing research and identifies common decision-making approaches.

---

# Problem Statement

Selecting a supplier is not simply choosing the company with the lowest price.

Organizations evaluate suppliers using several factors simultaneously such as quality, delivery performance, reliability, service, cost, and financial stability.

This makes supplier selection a multi-criteria decision-making problem.

---

# Methodology

The authors conducted a systematic literature review of previous supplier selection research.

They analyzed over one hundred published studies and categorized the techniques researchers used.

---

# Important Supplier Evaluation Factors

The paper identifies several factors commonly used when evaluating suppliers.

- Cost
- Product Quality
- Delivery Performance
- Reliability
- Service
- Financial Stability
- Supplier Risk
- Environmental Performance

---

# AI / Machine Learning Perspective

The paper discusses several intelligent techniques that have been applied in supplier selection.

Examples include

- Decision Trees
- Neural Networks
- Fuzzy Logic
- Genetic Algorithms

These methods attempt to improve supplier decision-making using historical information.

---

# Key Findings

Supplier selection should never rely on a single criterion.

Successful organizations evaluate suppliers using multiple performance indicators.

Different industries prioritize different factors depending on business requirements.

---

# Limitations

This paper focuses on reviewing supplier selection methods.

It does not build a predictive machine learning model.

It also does not specifically predict supplier risk.
